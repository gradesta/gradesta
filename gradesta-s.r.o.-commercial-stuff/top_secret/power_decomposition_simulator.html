Power Decomposition Simulator

Mathematical Equivalency: This simulator visualizes the decomposition:
2 · 3n-1 + Σ 3i · 22i-1 = 22n-1
where i goes from n-2 down to 0.

Features:
  • Shows each term in decimal, base 2, and base 3
  • Displays vertical tables of digits for addition visualization
  • Highlights how terms combine to form the final result
