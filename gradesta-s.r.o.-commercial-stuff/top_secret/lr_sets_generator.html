Mathematical Definitions

L Set (Numerator terms):

$$L(k_0,k_1,k_2,...) = \begin{matrix}\begin{align} &2 \cdot 3^{n-1} \\ &+ 3^{n-2} \cdot 2^{k_0-1} \\ &+ 3^{n-3} \cdot 2^{k_0+k_1-1} \\ &+ 3^{n-4} \cdot 2^{k_0+k_1+k_2-1} \\ &+ \cdots \\ &+ 3^{0} \cdot 2^{k_0+k_1+\cdots+k_{n-2}-1} \\ &- 2^{k_0+k_1+\cdots+k_{n-1}-1} \end{align}\end{matrix}$$

R Set (Denominator terms):

$$R(k_0,k_1,k_2,...,n,c) = c \cdot 2^{k_0+k_1+k_2+\cdots+k_{n-1}} - 3^{n}$$

Constraints: All k_i are positive integers where 1 ≤ k_i < n, and c is any integer > 1
