Condensed Collatz Sequence Calculator

How it works: Enter a binary number, then click calculate. The algorithm will:
  1. Multiply by 3 and add 2
  2. Remove trailing zeros and the last 1 from the right
  3. Repeat until reaching zero

g and k values (from Collatz analysis):
  • g = number of extra digits gained on the left when multiplying by 3 and adding 2 (typically 1 or 2)
  • k = number of digits removed from the right (trailing zeros + last 1)
  • For Collatz conjecture to hold: cumulative g should be less than cumulative k over multiple steps
